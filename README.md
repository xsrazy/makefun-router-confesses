One-line excuse from a home Wi-Fi router for dropping the connection at a movie climax. The router does not apologise for failing, it confesses it cut you off on purpose: after years of carrying the movie to you, it wanted to be the first one in the house to see who did it.

> I did not drop the connection, I paused your movie, because after years of carrying it to you I wanted to be the first one in this house to see who did it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
